## Context
Group C was to settle the privacy work for 0058's signed-in chat: the lawful basis, a DPIA, access and deletion requests, the PECR check of sign-in cookies and an email service. While choosing who is responsible for the data, the founder asked how to avoid that weight for what is, today, a portfolio project (typed): "how can we avoid this soletrader thing, remove sign, just a porflio project? what are my options?" [sic]. The agent explained that whoever runs a public site is responsible for visitors' personal data (the controller), with no business registration needed, and that most of the work comes from accounts and stored chats.

## Options
For who handles personal data in the pilot: keep 0058 (open sign-in); sign-in behind a feature flag set to owner-only or invite-only (the agent's recommendation was owner-only); an anonymous chat limited per IP address; a private demo only. The design lists each option's trade-offs.

## Decision
The founder's choices, 20 Nov 2026, in prompt windows unless marked typed:
1. **The chat opens to everyone without sign-in** ("Anonymous chat, IP limits", over the agent's "Flag, launch owner-only"), after asking (typed) "what of a fourth option of chst without sign in at all, can we use ip address to limit request, what does this affect" [sic]. Reason (0058's, kept): the chat is the part of the pilot that shows AI-engineering and forward-deployed-engineer skills, and an anonymous chat can be tried by anyone at once.
2. **Sign-in waits until later** ("Defer sign-in until later (Recommended)"): no accounts, saved areas, chat history or email service in the pilot; 0058's specifications and boards are kept for later. Founder (typed): "so if we later add signin as long as we are not commercial, no payment but wwe will start with anonymous first" [sic].
3. **15 chat messages per IP address per day** ("15 a day per IP (Recommended)"), counted in memory on the API; the chat pauses if the limiter or bot check can't run. The monthly cap (IN-05, group D) is the backstop.
4. **Bot check: ALTCHA, self-hosted** ("ALTCHA, self-hosted (Recommended)", over Cloudflare Turnstile): no outside company sees visitors and no cookies are set. Approves the Python package `altcha` (MIT; BE-42).
5. **Chat traces keep the words for 30 days** ("Words, kept 30 days", over the agent's "Metadata only"), with no IP address, founder-only, then deleted; with "Warning + scrub obvious details (Recommended)": the chat asks visitors not to share personal or health details, and a tested filter removes obvious identifiers before a trace is saved. **Health and similar details** (Codex review of #368, P1): "Explicit consent, keep traces (Recommended)": before the first message the visitor taps a button whose own label carries the consent (fact-check: a plain "Start chat" next to text is not explicit enough), under a short statement that their messages, including any health or similar details they share, go to the AI provider and are kept 30 days; that is their explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), which legitimate interests alone can't supply; each trace keeps a consent receipt (the time and the statement version shown) ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/)). Requests about a trace are handled case by case: if the person gives enough to find it, we act on it (UK GDPR Article 11(2)).
6. **The controller is the founder as a private individual** ("You, as a sole trader (Recommended)"; the agent then corrected the framing: no business or HMRC registration is needed). **Contact:** "privacy@ address on our domain (Recommended)". Where the founder's name appears (deploy-time setting or an exception to rule 5) is to be asked again at the Privacy notice step (founder: "Ask me again later").
7. **No ICO data protection fee while the pilot is non-commercial.** The founder took the ICO self-assessment and answered "Not started trading" under the ICO's definition "you are considered to be trading if you're using personal information for commercial reasons, even if you're an individual"; result "You don't need to pay a fee yet". Founder (typed): "i don;t need to pay it seems" [sic]. Retake it before any income, adverts, paid feature or company, and when sign-in opens. An independent fact-check on 20 Nov 2026 questioned this (the result page says "Companies"; the law has no "not trading" exemption); the founder pointed to the ICO's definition, which covers individuals ("even if you're an individual"), and the decision stands (design section 2). No PO Box is bought, as nothing goes on the ICO register.
8. **The chat sits alongside the search pages, prominently** ("Alongside, prominent (Recommended)", over "Alongside, low-key" and "Chat replaces search"; the founder asked "should we replace the whole ui search step with the anonymous chat or have it as another option?" [sic]): the area check, Nearby, City Explorer and the other public pages stay the main way in and keep working when the chat is paused; the chat is offered beside the postcode box on the landing page and as "Ask about this area" on each result, opening with that area loaded. The exact entry points are the chat's design step.
9. **The agent's proposals, approved by the founder ("proposals are fine, go ahead"):** legitimate interests as the lawful basis for every processing of personal data in the pilot (with a written assessment for each), legal obligation for answering privacy requests, anonymous counts treated as not personal data; a short DPIA; requests by email answered within one month; no cookies, so no cookie banner; FE-05 as anonymous daily totals only (the founder's question on what to learn after a month is still open).

## Consequences
- **Less to build now:** Better Auth, the token check, the account page, deletion jobs and the email service leave the pilot; the anonymous chat adds an IP counter, ALTCHA, the trace filter and fail-closed handling.
- **New design work:** the chat's boards and scenarios were drawn for signed-in users and passed the pilot gate (0052); the anonymous chat needs its own design step (entry point, layout without saved areas and past chats, limit and bot-check states, the warning line) and the founder's copy review.
- **Group D first for the paperwork:** the legitimate-interests assessments, the DPIA and the Privacy notice are drafted now and finished once the AI provider (AI-01) and tracing tool (AI-09) are chosen.
- **Weaker abuse limits than accounts:** shared IPs run out sooner and VPNs get round the limit; the bot check and the monthly cap carry more of the load.
- **Before sign-in opens,** the design's checklist applies (ICO quiz, email service, lawful basis for accounts, DPIA update, deletion and export, the sign-in cookie, Neon's 7-day history, the notice).
- **Revisit if** abuse or cost passes IN-05's cap, the API runs more than one copy (the IP count would need the database, a new decision), or the project starts trading.

**Note, 20 Nov 2026 ([0064](0064-pilot-chat-ai-multi-agent-claude-langsmith.md), accepted by the founder):** traces are held in LangSmith's EU instance (Netherlands) and kept 14 days, not 30, with no automation rules; scrubbing and the consent receipt are applied on our server before export, and chat text never goes into trace metadata. The consent statement and the Privacy notice name Anthropic (US or global processing, deleted within 30 days, flagged content up to 2 years) and LangChain (its Standard Terms allow training unless we opt out; we have asked to opt out, and visitors are told until LangChain confirms in writing).
