## Context
0063 opened the pilot's chat to anonymous visitors. Group D settles how it is built. The founder agreed to an intent classifier and asked for a multi-agent flow: "mainly to show employers i can build multi-agent systems but it also fits the application" [sic] (typed), then "make sure we are making the best decision". After the founder said the agent had been "mixing up decisions by not doing your due diligemce" [sic], an independent fact-check read every outside claim against its primary source, and the founder re-decided the choices it affected.

## Decision
The founder's choices, 20 Nov 2026, in prompt windows unless marked typed:
1. **Flow: "Refined B (Recommended)".** A classifier routes off-topic and verdict questions to fixed replies, simple area and council questions to one agent, and comparisons to a team: an orchestrator, parallel workers, a writer and a checker that confirms every figure came from a tool. At most 3 areas; plain code routes straight to the team where the page already knows it is a comparison. Supported by Anthropic's guidance (simplest first; multi-agent for breadth, about 15× the tokens).
2. **A comparison counts as 3 of the 15 daily messages** ("Counts as 3 messages (Recommended)").
3. **Provider: Anthropic** ("Go with Claude"; after the fact-check corrected retention to "within 30 days", flagged up to 2 years: "Keep Claude (Recommended)"). No training on our content; a hard spend limit; processing in the US or "global" under the DPA's UK Addendum. Approves the `anthropic` package (Apache-2.0).
4. **Models: "Haiku + Sonnet writer/checker"** (over the agent's "Start Haiku, upgrade if tests fail"): Haiku 5.5 for the classifier, the single agent, the orchestrator and the workers; Sonnet 5.5 for the writer and checker. About $20 a month at the assumed use (an estimate; the token sizes behind it were not recorded, so the AI-09 trial measures real token counts and replaces it).
5. **Monthly cap: £25** ("£25 a month (Recommended)"): our app reserves each call's worst-case cost against the daily and monthly budgets before the call and settles it afterwards (as 0023 keeps), and pauses the chat at 95%; Anthropic's workspace spend limits are the second guard (the visitor workspace at the cap, a test workspace with its own limit, and an organisation limit covering both); tests use that separate workspace. Daily cap, 21 Nov 2026 (founder, prompt window, after the pre-push review found that a per-IP limit does not cap a day's total): "Daily cap: £2.50 (Recommended)": when a day's spend reaches £2.50, a tenth of the monthly cap, the chat pauses until midnight and resumes the next day, so one busy day uses at most a tenth of the month. This meets the daily cap that doc 04, 0025 and 0051 ask for.
6. **Tracing: LangSmith, EU, free plan** ("LangSmith anyway", over the agent's "Ask LangChain; Phoenix if no", which the agent recommended because LangChain's Standard Terms allow training "unless Customer opts out"); then "Request opt-out, tell visitors (Recommended)". After the founder asked whether telling visitors was enough without the opt-out email, and what else could be done, the founder chose "C: words in Neon, rest in LangSmith (Recommended)": real visitors' words (messages, replies, tool inputs) stay in our own Neon table for 14 days (and are gone from Neon's 7-day history, 0060, a week after deletion, which the Privacy notice states), and LangSmith gets the same traces without words, linked by trace ID; test conversations and evaluations go to LangSmith in full. LangChain never receives visitors' words, so no training line is needed for visitors. Base traces kept 14 days, no automation rules, no payment method. Approves the `langsmith` package (MIT).
7. **The AI-09 trial** builds this flow in both Microsoft Agent Framework and LangGraph with test conversations, and the founder chooses the framework from the results (AI-04).

## Consequences
- **0051's provider safeguard** ("No training, short checks OK": inputs "kept only briefly for abuse checks") is met by Anthropic's no-training term and deletion within 30 days; flagged content kept up to 2 years is the one longer case, which the founder accepted with "Keep Claude (Recommended)" and the notice states.
- Simple questions stay cheap; comparisons carry the multi-agent showcase and its cost, limited by the 3-message count, the 3-area limit and the £25 cap.
- Chat text leaves the UK to Anthropic (US or global), named in the Privacy notice, the DPIA and the consent statement; LangChain (Netherlands, with US billing data) receives only words-free traces of real chats and is named as a processor.
- A small table, a daily delete job and saved queries are added to the build for real chats' words.
- **Revisit if** the AI-03 results show Haiku isn't good enough for a role, the cap is reached often, LangChain's terms change, or the trial shows the team adds no value over one agent.
